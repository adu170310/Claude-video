# Athlo launch film (30 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps, under 30 MB)

A launch film for Athlo in the style of an Apple product page, made entirely in Python (Pillow, numpy, scipy) and ffmpeg. Every frame and every sound is generated by code; the only inputs are the Athlo logo and the app screen recording in `assets/`.

```
./render.sh      # about 6 min on 4 cores -> out/athlo_launch_30s_1080x1920.mp4
```

## Structure (96 BPM, every text hit on the beat, no black frames)

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:04 | Already in motion on frame one: group chats, a spreadsheet and missed calls fly in and pile up. "Sport in Dubai is scattered." is readable by 0:00.4. Three letters slip slightly, then settle back into line. |
| 0:04 to 0:07 | The pile collapses into the real Athlo logo. The logo rises and a phone with the real app home page scrolls into view. |
| 0:07 to 0:20 | Five words, each with its own zoomed real-app detail. **Discover:** Places to play, with "13 places" counting up. **Book:** "Book courts…", with the Find a facility button tapped. **Play:** "What are you into?", with the Football chip tapped. **Train:** the Clubs row ("Coaches, classes and events") lights up. **Compete:** the Challenges row ("Free fitness challenges with prizes") lights up. |
| 0:20 to 0:22 | "Discover. Book. Play. Train. Compete." builds word by word. Upcoming words are never shown. |
| 0:22 to 0:30 | Logo and "Your sports life, in one place.", which then clears completely. Then "Join the Dubai Fitness 30x30" as the largest line, "Win a Whoop for playing any sport." and a big "Link in bio" button. |

## Rules the cut follows

- **Honest UI.** Every app image is a crop of the real screen recording (`src/prep.py`). Nothing is invented: no venue names, prices, booking buttons or leaderboards. The animations only replay what the app already does: the active-row tint from the real menu, the yellow active chip, a tap ripple, and a count-up that hands back to the real "13" pixels.
- **Clean transitions.** Each headline fully leaves before the next one appears. Nothing is blurred on exit, so there is no ghosting.
- **Safe zones.** Key text and buttons stay between y = 192 (the top tenth) and y = 1536 (the bottom fifth). The phone sits fully inside the frame.
- **Delivery.** -14 LUFS integrated with a true peak of -1 dBTP or lower. H.264 High, two-pass at 6.8 Mbps, AAC 192k, faststart.

## Files

- `src/timeline.py`: every cue time, shared by picture and sound.
- `src/prep.py`: cuts the logo off its white background (rings keep their colours, the navy wordmark turns white), crops the real UI details, and stitches the home page into one tall image for a smooth scroll.
- `src/film.py`: the frame renderer. `--still 12.5,30` writes single frames to `work/stills/` for quick review.
- `src/score.py`: the original ambient score (additive pads, sub bass, plucked arpeggio, soft kick) plus the sound design (hits, whooshes, UI taps, count ticks, risers, impacts).
- `src/master.py`: loudness, a true-peak limiter, and the two-pass encode with a size check.
