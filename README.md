# Athlo launch film (45 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps)

A launch film for Athlo in the style of an Apple product page, made entirely in Python (Pillow, numpy, scipy) and ffmpeg. Every frame and every sound is generated by code; the only inputs are the Athlo logo and the app screen recording in `assets/`.

```
./render.sh      # about 10 min on 4 cores -> out/athlo_launch_45s_1080x1920.mp4
```

## Structure (96 BPM, every text hit on the beat)

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:05 | Black. "Sport in Dubai is scattered." The letters of "scattered" drift apart. |
| 0:05 to 0:15 | Group chats, a spreadsheet and missed calls float in depth, then collapse into one phone running the real Athlo app (scrolling home page from the recording). "One clean app." |
| 0:15 to 0:23 | Discover. Book. Play. Train. Compete. One word per beat; "Compete." in the brand gradient. |
| 0:23 to 0:36 | Four phones slide in: Find a court, Book it, Join a game, Climb the leaderboard. Each screen has a small interaction: typing, slot tap, join, rank climb. |
| 0:36 to 0:45 | Real logo, "Your sports life, in one place.", "Join the Dubai Fitness 30x30." and a "Link in bio" pill. |

## Files

- `src/timeline.py`: every cue time, shared by picture and sound.
- `src/prep.py`: cuts the logo off its white background (rings keep their colours, the navy wordmark turns white), crops photos from the recording, and stitches the home page into one tall image for a smooth scroll.
- `src/film.py`: the frame renderer. `--still 12.5,30` writes single frames to `work/stills/` for quick review.
- `src/score.py`: the original ambient score (additive pads, sub bass, plucked arpeggio, soft kick) plus the sound design (text hits, whooshes, UI taps, risers, impacts).
- `src/master.py`: gains the mix to -14 LUFS, runs a true-peak limiter at -1.5 dBFS (4x oversampled), then muxes H.264 High with AAC 320k and faststart.

## Notes

- The "Places to play" header, logo bar, photos and home page are taken from the real app recording. The booking, join-a-game and leaderboard screens are mock-ups in the app's own palette and type, because the recording doesn't include those flows. The names and numbers on them are placeholders.
- The music is synthesised here, so it is original and carries no licence or Content ID risk.

# Athlo 15 s Instagram Reel (Remotion + react-three-fiber)

`reel/` is a separate 15 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps reel built with Remotion and `@remotion/three`. The glass (transmission + roughness), depth of field, bloom and film grain are real WebGL materials and post-processing passes. The only inputs are the real logo and the real app screen recording.

```
cd reel && npm install
python3 scripts/prep.py                  # logo matte + real app screens -> public/
scripts/sheet.sh                         # one approval still per beat -> work/stills/
node scripts/render.mjs video            # picture -> work/reel_picture.mp4 (GL=swangle on CPU-only machines)
python3 audio/score.py                   # original synthesised score + sound design
python3 audio/master.py                  # -14 LUFS, true peak < -1.5 dBTP, mux -> out/athlo_reel_15s_1080x1920.mp4
```

Cue times live in `reel/src/timeline.json` and drive both picture and sound. Type is Inter at its Display optical size (opsz 32), weight 500, instanced from the OFL Inter variable font.
